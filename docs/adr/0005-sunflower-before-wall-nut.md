# The Sunflower is built before the Wall-nut

Level 1-1's pacing is balanced against a real economy rather than against sky Sun alone, so the Sunflower is built first and the Wall-nut second. Level 1-1's Wave spacing was chosen at ticket 08 on the assumption that a Peashooter is the only thing Sun is ever spent on, and a Sunflower invalidates that assumption: a schedule tuned before the economy exists is tuned against a lie.

This leaves eating purposeless for one release longer, which `CHANGELOG.md` already records from ticket 05 as a gap — nothing is worth being eaten *for* until something blocks a Row. The Sunflower is taken first anyway, because it is the only plant so far that needs a new mechanism: Sun made at a plant rather than by the sky. The Wall-nut needs none, since a plant that never shoots and a plant that is food are both already expressed, so it is cheap whenever it is reached. Building it first would mean tuning a defence against a Peashooter-only economy, and the Sunflower exists to invalidate exactly that.

The trade is recorded rather than hidden: a balance pass on Level 1-1 follows the Sunflower rather than preceding the Wall-nut, and that pass is where the level's numbers are settled by playing rather than by reading.
